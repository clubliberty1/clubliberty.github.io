What We Do

Turn ideas into action.

Club Liberty chapters combine civic programming, student leadership, community service, and larger humanitarian projects. Our goal is simple: give young people meaningful ways to participate, lead, and make a measurable difference.

Our Programs

Four areas. One mission.

Club Liberty chapters are built around a balance of civic involvement and direct service. Chapters are encouraged to adapt their programming to their own campus and community while remaining committed to Club Liberty's broader mission.

01

Civic Programs

Debates, guest speakers, voter registration, and opportunities for students to participate in civic life.

02

Chapter Leadership

Training students to organize, fundraise, communicate, and lead their own campus chapter.

03

Community Service

Local projects that address practical needs in the communities surrounding our chapters.

04

Humanitarian Projects

Larger initiatives serving children, families, and communities in the United States and abroad.

Civic Programs

Create spaces worth participating in.

Each chapter can build a calendar of civic programs based on the interests and needs of its campus.

The goal is not simply to gather people who already agree. It is to create opportunities for students to think, participate, speak, listen, and engage.

Debates

Student debates and structured discussions on political, social, cultural, and public policy questions.

Guest Speakers

Bringing leaders from law, government, business, faith, public service, and other fields directly to students.

Voter Registration

Nonpartisan registration efforts that help eligible students participate in elections.

Civic Volunteer Work

Connecting students with opportunities to contribute to their campus and local community.

Chapter Leadership

Train leaders. Give them room to lead.

Club Liberty provides chapter leaders with guidance, resources, and organizational support, while allowing each chapter meaningful control over its own campus programming.

President Training

Prepare chapter presidents to manage teams, organize meetings, plan events, communicate effectively, and represent Club Liberty well.

Chapter Autonomy

Allow campus leaders to make decisions based on the needs and opportunities unique to their university.

Fundraising

Teach chapters how to raise funds for their own events and projects while also supporting larger Club Liberty initiatives.

Event & Project Management

Develop practical experience in planning, budgeting, outreach, logistics, teamwork, and execution.

Community Service

Serve the community around your chapter.

Every chapter should look beyond campus and find practical ways to serve people nearby. Projects can change depending on local needs, partnerships, resources, and chapter size.

Food Drives

Collecting food and working with community partners to support individuals and families experiencing need.

School Supplies

Collecting backpacks, supplies, and educational materials for students and families.

Support for Mothers

Baby clothes, diapers, supplies, and other practical support for pregnant women, mothers, and young families.

School Outreach

Speaking with younger students about leadership, civic responsibility, opportunity, and service.

The Chapter Standard

Debate and service belong together.

Club Liberty chapters are expected to participate in both civic programming and community service.

A chapter should not become only a discussion club, just as it should not abandon civic engagement entirely.

Practice what you defend.

If our members advocate for stronger families, they should find ways to support families.

If they defend the dignity of life, they should also care about mothers and children who are already here.

Ideas are more credible when they are followed by responsibility.

Humanitarian Projects

Build projects larger than one campus.

As Club Liberty grows, larger projects will bring together chapters, volunteers, donors, churches, community partners, and other organizations.

These initiatives are intended to serve children and families both in the United States and internationally, with a particular long-term interest in communities throughout Latin America.

Food Assistance

Food distributions, community meals, and future food-shelter projects for children and families.

Children's Events

Community events with meals, toys, games, activities, and opportunities for children and families to gather.

Education

School supplies, educational resources, partnerships, and future projects supporting schools and learning environments.

Housing & Community Projects

Long-term initiatives aimed at improving living conditions and helping communities build sustainable support systems.

How Projects Grow

Local action. Broader impact.

Club Liberty is designed so students can begin serving at the chapter level while contributing to increasingly ambitious projects as the organization grows.

STEP 01

Campus Chapters

Chapters organize their own civic events, volunteer opportunities, fundraising, and local service projects.

STEP 02

Regional Projects

Multiple chapters and partners can work together on larger projects serving communities throughout a region.

STEP 03

National & International Work

Larger Club Liberty initiatives can bring resources and volunteers together for humanitarian projects beyond any single chapter.

Independent Civic Engagement

Participate without becoming a campaign organization.

Club Liberty does not endorse or campaign for political candidates.

Our civic programming instead focuses on issues, education, debate, voter participation, public service, and developing informed citizens.
Independent by design.

Club Liberty is guided by its mission and principles rather than loyalty to a candidate, campaign, or political party.

Faith & Service

Serve with compassion and dignity.

Club Liberty's Christian faith informs the spirit in which we serve. Some projects may include opportunities to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ, while every project should seek to treat people with compassion, generosity, dignity, and respect.
