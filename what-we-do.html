What We Do

Learn. Lead. Serve.

Club Liberty exists to turn civic engagement into meaningful action. We help young people understand the issues shaping their communities, develop the skills to lead, and then put those principles into practice through service.

Our Model

Civic engagement should lead somewhere.

We believe strong communities need informed citizens, capable leaders, and people willing to serve. Club Liberty brings those three responsibilities together.

01

Learn

Understand civic institutions, public issues, competing ideas, and the responsibilities that come with citizenship.

02

Lead

Develop the confidence, judgment, organization, and practical skills necessary to take responsibility and lead others well.

03

Serve

Turn conviction into action by serving real people, strengthening communities, and responding to practical needs.

Civic Engagement

Learn to think. Learn to participate.

Club Liberty chapters create opportunities for students to engage seriously with public life.

Debate matters to us because strong convictions should be tested, defended, challenged, and refined. But civic engagement goes beyond debate.

Debates

Structured conversations where students engage difficult issues, defend ideas, and learn to understand opposing arguments.

Guest Speakers

Events that bring experienced voices in public service, law, policy, faith, business, and civic life directly to students.

Voter Registration

Nonpartisan efforts encouraging eligible students to register, participate, and take their civic responsibilities seriously.

Volunteer Work

Civic engagement should connect students to the actual needs of the communities around them.

Leadership Development

Give students responsibility.

Club Liberty chapters are not designed to simply follow instructions from above. We want chapter leaders to understand the mission, develop their own leadership ability, and take meaningful responsibility for the communities they lead.

Chapter Leadership

Train chapter presidents and leadership teams to organize meetings, events, service projects, and campus initiatives effectively.

Local Ownership

Chapters operate within Club Liberty's mission and values while retaining room to respond to the needs and culture of their own campuses.

Fundraising

Teach chapter leaders how to raise support for local chapter activities while contributing to broader Club Liberty projects.

Organization

Develop practical skills in communication, event planning, teamwork, project management, and community outreach.

Serving Communities

Start where you are.

Our chapters should make a visible difference in the communities around them.

Service projects may vary from chapter to chapter, but the principle remains the same: leadership carries a responsibility to help people.

Food Drives

Collecting and distributing food for individuals and families experiencing need.

School Supplies

Helping students and families access the supplies they need to learn and succeed.

Supporting Mothers

Collecting baby supplies and other practical resources for pregnant women, mothers, and young families.

Speaking at Schools

Encouraging younger students to value leadership, civic responsibility, service, and opportunity.

Conviction In Action

Practice what you defend.

Club Liberty believes ideas come with responsibilities.

It is not enough to advocate for a principle and then ignore the people affected by it. Conviction should produce service, responsibility, and compassion.

If we defend life, we should help support life.

For example, a pro-life conviction should not end with a political position on abortion. It should also mean supporting pregnant women, helping mothers, caring for children, and strengthening families.

The same principle applies across our work: belief should be followed by action.

Humanitarian Work

Service beyond borders.

Club Liberty's long-term vision extends beyond university campuses and beyond the United States.

We seek to serve children and families in communities facing poverty and limited access to basic resources, including communities throughout Latin America.

Food Assistance

Supporting children and families through food distribution and future food-shelter initiatives.

Children's Events

Creating events with games, activities, meals, toys, and opportunities for children to experience joy, dignity, and community.

Education & Schools

Supporting educational needs through school supplies, community partnerships, and future educational projects.

Housing & Community Projects

Working toward projects that improve living conditions and strengthen communities over the long term.

Faith In Action

Serve in a way that reflects Christ.

Club Liberty's Christian faith shapes why and how we serve.

Not every Club Liberty event is a preaching event, and service is never valuable only when someone shares our beliefs.

But we are not ashamed of our faith. Some projects may create opportunities to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ, and every project should reflect the compassion, dignity, generosity, courage, and love we see in His example.

Is this Christlike?

That question remains one of the standards by which we seek to evaluate our leadership, conduct, and service.

Independent Civic Engagement

Principles before parties.

Club Liberty is independent of political parties and does not endorse or campaign for candidates.

We encourage members to develop their own convictions, participate responsibly in civic life, and engage seriously with the issues affecting their communities.
Engage without becoming beholden.

Our goal is to develop principled citizens and leaders — not to become an extension of a political party or campaign.
