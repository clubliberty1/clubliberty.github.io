What We Do

Turn ideas into action.

Club Liberty develops young leaders by combining civic engagement, practical leadership, community service, and larger humanitarian projects.

Civic Programs

Learn to engage. Learn to lead.

Club Liberty chapters create spaces where students can debate ideas, hear from guest speakers, participate in civic life, and learn how institutions actually work.

We encourage serious discussion, respectful disagreement, and participation rooted in principles rather than party loyalty.

Chapters may also organize nonpartisan voter registration, civic volunteer work, educational programming, and community events.

Chapter Leadership

Give students real responsibility.

Club Liberty chapters are not meant to simply receive instructions from a national organization.

We want chapter presidents and leadership teams to develop the skills required to actually lead: planning events, raising funds, building partnerships, organizing volunteers, and managing projects.

Chapters retain meaningful local autonomy while operating within Club Liberty's broader mission and standards.

Community Service

Serve the people around you.

Every chapter should find practical ways to support the community around it.

That can include food drives, collecting school supplies, supporting pregnant women and mothers, helping families, volunteering locally, or speaking with younger students.

Service does not have to begin with a massive project. It begins by noticing a need and deciding to do something about it.

The Chapter Standard

Debate and service belong together.

We do not want Club Liberty chapters to become organizations that only talk about what they believe.

If we defend a principle, we should be willing to put that principle into practice.
Practice what you defend.

If we defend life, our responsibility does not end with an argument about abortion. We should also be willing to support mothers, children, and families who need help.

Humanitarian Projects

Build beyond the campus.

As Club Liberty grows, our service work should grow with it.

Our long-term humanitarian work includes food assistance, events for children, educational support, housing and community projects, and support for families.

We intend to develop projects in the United States and internationally, with particular focus on communities in Latin America.

How We Grow

From campus to community.

National & International Work

As the network grows, Club Liberty can support larger humanitarian projects in the United States and abroad.

Independent Civic Engagement

Engage without becoming a party machine.

Club Liberty is rooted in conservative principles while remaining institutionally independent from political parties.

We encourage students to think critically, participate in civic life, understand public policy, and defend their convictions.

Club Liberty does not endorse or campaign for political candidates.

Faith & Service

Serve with dignity and compassion.

Our Christian faith informs the spirit of our service.

Some humanitarian projects may create opportunities to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Regardless of the project, every person we serve should be treated with dignity, compassion, and respect.
