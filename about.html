About Club Liberty

Leadership means more than having a voice. It means serving.

Founded in 2026 at California Lutheran University, Club Liberty was created to develop a generation of leaders who believe civic engagement should lead to meaningful action.

Who We Are

We believe leadership is service.

Club Liberty is an independent civic organization grounded in freedom, service, conviction, civic responsibility, and the belief that leadership carries an obligation to serve others.


We bring young people together to engage with civic life, develop as leaders, exchange ideas, and translate those principles into direct service in their communities.

Civic engagement should not stop at conversation.
Our Story

From a campus idea to a larger mission.

Club Liberty began after an unsuccessful effort to establish a chapter of another national civic organization at California Lutheran University. That experience created an opportunity to think more deeply about what a civic organization should actually accomplish.

Rather than simply recreating another political club, the vision began to expand.

Founder Tristan Silva returned to an idea that had shaped him personally: his mother's dream of creating places where children and families in Mexico could receive food, care, and hear the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

That dream became part of the foundation for Club Liberty's broader mission — to connect civic engagement and leadership with direct service to people.

Club Liberty was founded in 2026 at California Lutheran University and soon expanded with a chapter at Azusa Pacific University.

Our Faith

Guided by the example of Jesus Christ.

Club Liberty is guided by Christian values and the teachings and example of Jesus Christ.

We do not believe faith should be reduced to slogans. We believe it should shape how we lead, how we serve, how we treat people, and how we respond even when we disagree.
Is this Christlike?

That question represents the standard we strive to bring into our decisions, relationships, projects, and leadership.

Our Values

The principles that guide us.

Freedom

Defending the freedom of people to think, speak, worship, participate, and pursue opportunity.

Service

Leadership should produce action that benefits the people and communities around us.

Conviction

Standing firmly for principles while treating others with dignity and respect.

Leadership

Developing people willing to take responsibility rather than wait for others to act.

Civic Responsibility

Citizenship carries responsibilities as well as rights.

Civil Discourse

Difficult ideas should be discussed openly, thoughtfully, and without dehumanizing those who disagree.

Human Dignity

Every person deserves to be treated as someone of inherent worth.

Faith

Our understanding of service and leadership is shaped by the teachings and example of Jesus Christ.

Independent By Principle

Principles before parties.

Club Liberty is an independent civic organization. We are not affiliated with or beholden to any political party.


Our worldview is rooted in conservative principles including freedom, personal responsibility, strong communities, civic participation, and respect for human dignity.


We believe ideas should be judged on their principles, their consequences, and whether they genuinely serve the people affected by them.

Our Vision

Build leaders. Serve communities. Reach beyond borders.

Club Liberty's vision extends beyond university campuses. We seek to build a network of chapters that develops young leaders while creating meaningful service projects in the United States and internationally.

Our long-term work includes food assistance, shelters, educational initiatives, housing and community projects, and humanitarian efforts serving children and families in need — including communities throughout Latin America.

Our Founders
