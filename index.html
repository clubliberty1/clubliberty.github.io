Who We Are

Ideas become meaningful when they become action.

Club Liberty is a student-led organization focused on developing young leaders through civic engagement, community service, and meaningful action.


Through university chapters and community initiatives, we create opportunities for students to lead, serve, and contribute to the communities around them.
