Who We Are

Ideas become meaningful when they become action.

Club Liberty is an independent, student-led civic organization focused on developing young leaders through civic engagement, community service, faith, and meaningful action.

We believe civic engagement should be more than discussion. Leadership means taking responsibility, showing up, and serving people directly.

We believe leadership is service.

Our Philosophy

Public service should actually serve people.

Politics and civic engagement matter because decisions affect real people. For us, participation is only the beginning.


Club Liberty exists to connect leadership with service. We want young people to learn about civic life, develop the confidence to lead, and put those values into practice by improving the communities around them.

Learn

Understand civic life, public issues, institutions, and the responsibilities of citizenship.

Lead

Develop the confidence, judgment, discipline, and practical skills required to take responsibility.

Serve

Put principles into practice through community service and meaningful action.
